Role 1: AI-Assisted Semantic Discovery

  • Purpose: Connects related management statements, footnotes, and financial tables across multi-period filings even when terminology changes completely.
  • Why Exact Keyword Matching Fails:
    • False Negatives (Missed Findings): CEO phrasing like "compute is revenue" shares zero keywords with "Changes in accounts receivable $(22,346)M".
    • False Positives (Boilerplate Collisions): Earnings releases repeat identical sentence templates ("Fourth-quarter [Segment] revenue was up X% from the previous quarter...") across unrelated segments like Automotive, Gaming, and ProViz.

Role 2: Deterministic Python Verification

  • Purpose: Executes 100% of YoY/QoQ growth calculations, basis-point margin checks, Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) math, and 4-category classification rules in unit-tested Python.
  • Zero Hallucinated Math: Language models never calculate or judge numerical discrepancies in ARGUS. Every comparison displays its exact Python formula and raw inputs.
